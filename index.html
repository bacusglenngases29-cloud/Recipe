Pork Sisig Recipe

INGREDIENTS

  • 500 g pork belly (liempo)
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2-3 pcs calamansi
  • 1-2 pcs chili (optional)
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp mayonnaise (optional)
  • 1 tsp vinegar
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 tbsp cooking oil
  • Water for boiling

INSTRUCTIONS STEP BY STEP

  1. Boil the pork in water for 30-45 minutes or until tender.
  2. Drain the pork and let it cool slightly.
  3. Grill or fry the pork until browned and crispy.
  4. Chop the pork into small pieces.
  5. Heat the oil and saute the garlic and half of the onion.
  6. Add the chopped pork, soy sauce, vinegar, and chili. Mix well.
  7. Cook for a few minutes and season with salt and pepper.
  8. Add the calamansi juice and mayonnaise if desired.
  9. Top with the remaining chopped onion and serve hot.
